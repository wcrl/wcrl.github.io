Electronics

As soon as your CAD is complete, please submit it to our executive board for review. If your design meets our minimum requirements, you will be given a kit to start assembling the electronic components of your bot.

Kits

Your kit will include:

  • WCRL Custom PCB
    • Connects all electrical components
    • Various smaller electronic components (resistors, voltage regulators, etc.) will also be included
  • ESP32
    • Microcontroller that you will program to control the robot
  • N20 Brushed motors (x2)
    • For your drivetrain
  • One of the following
    • EMAX ECO II Series 2306 BLDC motor & ESC
      • For spinner weapons
    • MG996R Servo motor & Buck Converter
      • For flipper weapons
